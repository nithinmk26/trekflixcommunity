About Nandi Hills Sunrise Trek

Located about 60 km north of Bangalore, Nandi Hills (Nandidurga) is an ancient hill fortress offering one of South India's most breathtaking sunrise panoramas. Famous for its thick morning mist and carpet of clouds, the hill is steeped in rich history associated with the Cholas, Hoysalas, and Tipu Sultan.

This early morning trek lets you bypass regular traffic, climb historic stone pathways under the stars, and witness the magic of dawn unfolding with a cup of hot chai and breakfast afterwards.
