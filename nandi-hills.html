Nandi Hills Sunrise Trek

Nandi Hills, Karnataka

5/5 (1 Review) 1 Night 1 Day Group size: 25

About this trek

Want to begin your day above the clouds? Join us on the Nandi Hills Sunrise Trek and witness one of the most iconic sunrises near Bangalore! A short, scenic adventure perfect for beginners, nature lovers and early risers.

Nandi Hills is a popular hill station near Bangalore known for its misty mornings and golden sunrises. Around 60 km from Bangalore, it's ideal for a quick getaway with approximately 1.5 hours of travel time. The trek is easy to moderate in difficulty, suitable for beginners and all age groups. Best time to visit is September to May.

Highlights

  • Experience the magnificent sunrise over the Nandi Hills in all its glory
  • Embark on a trek through breathtaking landscapes, led by knowledgeable and experienced guides
  • Benefit from convenient round-trip transportation from Bangalore
  • All necessary permits are pre-arranged for a hassle-free adventure
  • Ideal for nature enthusiasts, thrill-seekers, and anyone yearning for a unique experience
