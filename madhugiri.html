About Madhugiri Fort Trek

Rising dramatically to 3,930 feet, Madhugiri Betta is the second largest monolithic rock in Asia (after Savandurga). The hill derives its name from the honeybee colonies that nested on its slopes for centuries.

The hill was fortified by Raja Hire Gowda in the 17th century and later reinforced by Hyder Ali. The trek takes you through massive stone gateways, granaries, and natural water pools before reaching the exposed granite incline and the summit fort ruins.
