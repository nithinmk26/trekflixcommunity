Madhugiri Fort Sunrise Trek

Madhugiri Betta, Karnataka

New Trek 1 Night 1 Day Group size: 25

About this trek

Looking for a thrilling weekend escape from Bengaluru? Embark on the Madhugiri Hills Trek, a perfect combination of history, challenge, and scenic beauty. Just a few hours from the city, Madhugiri offers a unique trekking experience as you scale Asia's second-largest monolithic hill, surrounded by rugged terrain and panoramic views.

The trail begins at the base of Madhugiri town and gradually becomes more demanding as you ascend the monolith. With each turn, you'll find scenic photo spots, ancient structures, and thrilling rock paths that test your agility and endurance. Once at the top, the effort pays off with panoramic vistas and a sense of achievement like no other.

Highlights

  • Embark on a thrilling trek up Madhugiri, Asia's second-largest monolith, known for its steep rock faces and stunning views
  • Explore the historic Madhugiri Fort, featuring ancient architecture, battlements, and watchtowers from the Vijayanagara era
  • Challenge yourself with a moderately difficult climb, guided safely by an experienced and professional trek leader
  • Enjoy seamless travel with convenient pick-up and drop-off from multiple Bangalore locations
