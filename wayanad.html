Wayanad Weekend Tour

Wayanad, Kerala, India

5/5 (3 Reviews) 2 Day 2 Night Group size: 35

About this tour

Unplug and dive into the lush beauty of Wayanad, Kerala’s adventure and nature haven. From waterfalls and thrilling glass bridges to ancient caves and dam-side serenity, this 2-day Wayanad Tour promises the perfect mix of thrill and tranquility for your long weekend.

Wayanad is known for its misty mountains, waterfalls and historic caves. The trip is ideal for groups, couples and explorers, with the best time to visit from October to May. The road distance from Bangalore is approximately 280 km and takes around 7 to 8 hours.

Trip Highlights

  • Soochipara Waterfalls
  • 900 Kandi Glass Bridge
  • Adventure activities like ziplining, sky cycling and giant swing
  • Banasura Sagar Dam
  • Edakkal Caves
