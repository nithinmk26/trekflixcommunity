About the Wayanad Weekend Tour

Unplug and dive into the lush natural beauty of Wayanad, Kerala's premier hill station and adventure haven. From roaring forest waterfalls and thrilling sky bridges to ancient Stone Age caves and Asia's second largest earth dam, this curated 2-day getaway promises the ideal harmony of excitement, relaxation, and cultural exploration.

Located approximately 280 km from Bangalore, Wayanad offers cool misty breezes, sprawling spice estates, and serene nature trails. Perfect for solo travelers, friend circles, couples, and weekend explorers!

Trip Highlights

🌉

900 Kandi Glass Bridge

Walk high above the lush green canopy on South India's most famous transparent sky bridge.

🌊

Soochipara Waterfalls

Trek down through dense tea estates to bathe and relax near thunderous three-tiered cascades.

🗿

Edakkal Prehistoric Caves

Hike up Ambukuthi Mala to marvel at ancient Neolithic petroglyphs dating back 6,000+ years.

🏞️

Banasura Sagar Dam

Visit India's largest earthen dam surrounded by stunning emerald islands and misty hills.

🧗

Adventure Activities

Experience adrenaline-pumping ziplines, giant swings, and sky cycling above valley views.

🔥

Homestay & Kerala Dinner

Relish warm hospitality, authentic Kerala dinners, and cozy group bonding with fellow travelers.
