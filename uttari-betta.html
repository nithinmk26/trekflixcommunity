About Uttari Betta (Huthridurga) Trek

Uttari Betta, also known as Huthridurga, is a fortified hill in Kunigal taluk, approximately 75 km from Bangalore. Built by Kempe Gowda in the 16th century, the hill features seven fortified stone gateways leading to the summit temple of Lord Shiva (Shankaresvara).

The trail passes through lush greenery, natural caves, and stone staircases. At the top, trekkers are rewarded with a panoramic 360-degree view of dawn breaking through blanket clouds. After the descent, enjoy a delicious South Indian breakfast and a lake kayaking session!
