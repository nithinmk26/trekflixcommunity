Uttari Betta Sunrise Trek + Kayaking

Huthridurga (Santhepete), Karnataka

5/5 (6 Reviews) 1 Night 1 Day Group size: 45 / 30 / 20 / 12

About this trek

Looking for a quick weekend getaway from Bengaluru? Experience the beauty of the Uttari Betta Sunrise Trek, a perfect blend of adventure, nature and tranquility. Just a short drive from the city, this trek offers a refreshing escape into Karnataka’s breathtaking landscapes.

Trek through lush trails, visit an ancient stone fort and the Lord Shankareshwar Temple, and witness a magical sunrise from the summit. After a self-funded breakfast, unwind with a kayaking session before heading back to Bangalore.

Highlights

  • 5 km scenic trek through rocky terrain and serene landscapes
  • Historical fort and Lord Shankareshwar Temple at the summit
  • Stunning sunrise views over the surrounding valleys
  • Kayaking session after breakfast at 9:00 AM
  • Convenient pickup and drop-off from multiple Bangalore locations
